## Professional Framing: Business Metrics & Impact

### Key Business Verbs to Use:
- **Reduce**: costs, errors, manual work, time-to-market, technical debt
- **Increase**: revenue, capacity, efficiency, retention, contract value
- **Enable**: market expansion, new business models, scalability, strategic initiatives
- **Improve**: patient outcomes, provider satisfaction, operational efficiency, compliance posture
- **Drive**: adoption, engagement, revenue cycle performance, clinical quality
- **Optimize**: resource utilization, workflows, capacity planning, margin
- **Scale**: operations, platform capabilities, customer base, infrastructure
- **Eliminate**: bottlenecks, manual processes, errors, friction

### How to Frame Your Work (Examples):

**Instead of**: "I built a pharmacy fulfillment workflow"
**Say**: "I **eliminated** prescription routing errors while **increasing** throughput to 200+ monthly prescriptions, which **enabled** the platform to **scale** to 48-state fulfillment coverage"

**Instead of**: "I integrated PayTheory billing"
**Say**: "I **reduced** billing inflexibility that was limiting business model options, **enabling** a $499/year subscription offering that **drove** new contract value and **improved** customer retention"

**Instead of**: "I managed a team of engineers"
**Say**: "I **coordinated** cross-functional execution across 4 engineers and a CTO to **deliver** a multi-tenant SaaS platform in 9 months, **generating** $12,475 in first-month revenue and **positioning** the company for **market expansion**"

### Bottom-Line Metrics They Care About:

1. **Revenue Impact**:
   - Contract value (Chris's behavioral health expansion = $8M+)
   - Collections rate (their pricing model depends on this)
   - Market expansion (new customer segments = new revenue)

2. **Operational Efficiency**:
   - Provider utilization (empty appointment slots = lost revenue)
   - Administrative burden (time spent on scheduling vs. patient care)
   - No-show rates (predictive modeling to reduce)

3. **Customer Outcomes**:
   - Patient access (time to appointment)
   - Provider satisfaction (workflow efficiency)
   - Practice growth (their customers need to grow to pay more)

4. **Technical Debt & Scalability**:
   - Platform performance at scale (100K+ providers)
   - Integration complexity (they have a marketplace ecosystem)
   - Compliance posture (HIPAA, TEFCA, etc.)

## Professional Language Cheat Sheet

### Talking About Your Work:

| Instead of... | Say... |
|--------------|--------|
| "I worked on..." | "I led..." / "I drove..." / "I delivered..." |
| "We built..." | "I coordinated a team of X to build..." |
| "It was successful" | "It generated $X revenue / eliminated Y errors / improved Z by N%" |
| "I helped with..." | "I owned..." / "I was accountable for..." |
| "I learned..." | "I developed expertise in..." |
| "It was hard" | "The complexity required..." / "The constraints demanded..." |
| "I think..." | "Based on my experience with X, I believe..." |

### Talking About Impact:

- **Revenue**: "Generated $12,475 in first-month revenue" / "Enabled $499/year model" / "Drove $8M+ in new contract value"
- **Efficiency**: "Eliminated prescription routing errors" / "Reduced manual routing time by X%" / "Increased throughput to 200+ monthly prescriptions"
- **Scale**: "Supported 48-state fulfillment" / "Served 100K+ providers" / "Managed platform for 10K+ customers"
- **Quality**: "Achieved zero routing errors" / "Maintained HIPAA compliance" / "Ensured 99.9% uptime"
- **Speed**: "Delivered in 9 months" / "Reduced time-to-market by X%" / "Accelerated release cycle"

## Red Flags to Avoid

### Don't:
- ❌ Badmouth previous employers (even if true)
- ❌ Claim expertise you don't have (be honest about learning curves)
- ❌ Talk only about what you built, not why it mattered
- ❌ Get too technical too fast (read the room - is he interested?)
- ❌ Dismiss UX as "just design" (Chris cares deeply about human-centered design)
- ❌ Pretend you know athenaHealth's internal challenges (ask questions instead)
- ❌ Focus only on your career growth (balance with what you can contribute)

### Do:
- ✅ Ask thoughtful questions that show you've done research
- ✅ Be honest about what you don't know but are eager to learn
- ✅ Tie your experience to their business outcomes
- ✅ Show curiosity about their challenges and constraints
- ✅ Demonstrate respect for healthcare domain complexity
- ✅ Balance technical depth with business acumen
- ✅ Listen more than you talk (it's a conversation, not a pitch)
